### Features for Visitors:
* **Drag-and-Drop / Click-to-Upload**: Easily analyze any image (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`) directly in your browser.
* **Real-Time Analysis**: Pixels are scanned and processed locally in the browser with zero loading lag.
* **Animated Chart Display**: Dynamic horizontal bar charts animate values from 0% to their target percentages upon upload.
* **Detailed Stats Table**: Shows exact pixel counts and formatted percentages for all 11 color categories.
* **Fully Responsive**: Optimally styled for both desktop and mobile viewports.
